FAQ

Are the real headlines actually real?

Yes. Each one is checked against news reports, and the source link appears right after you answer.

Where do the fake ones come from?

We write them. They’re meant to sound believable, but none of them happened. They’re never presented as news.

Does it save my streak?

Your streak and best scores are saved in your browser on this device. No account and no tracking.

Is this connected to GTA VI?

No. It’s a fan-made game inspired by Florida’s reputation. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
